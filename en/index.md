# Privacy Policy for Anam

**Effective date:** July 18, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 18, 2026

[العربية](../ar/) · [Language index](../)

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of **Anam** (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Anam iOS app. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the App.

## 1. Scope and core privacy approach

Anam helps users manage farms, livestock, animal profiles, breeding, health, movement, weight, and production records. The App does not require an account and we do not operate a server that receives or stores your farm and livestock records. Those records remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Information you choose to enter may include:

- Farm names, locations, and notes
- Animal tags, names, species, breed, sex, age, status, parent tags, and breeding information
- Health, vaccination, treatment, movement, weight, production, and breeding records
- Permit, certificate, quarantine, and other livestock-related notes
- Optional animal photos

This information is stored locally using Apple platform storage. We do not receive, view, sell, or use these livestock records for advertising.

## 3. Photos, imports, and exports

When you select an animal photo, Apple’s photo picker gives the App access only to the item you choose. The selected image is stored with the local animal record.

You may import or export CSV files and other supported documents. Imported data is processed on your device. Exported files are saved or shared only where you direct them. After export, the destination app, storage provider, or recipient controls that copy, so protect exported files appropriately.

## 4. Advertising and consent

The free version may display ads using Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The App also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required.

The App requests ads only after UMP reports that ads may be requested. Ads in the current version are configured as non-personalized. Anam does not send farm records, animal records, photos, or imported CSV content to Google.

Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen it in **Settings → Privacy & Support → Privacy Options**. You may also manage relevant device permissions in iOS Settings. The availability and wording of consent controls may depend on your region.

## 5. Information Google may process

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information needed to serve, measure, and protect ads, including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate coarse location
- Device or app identifiers
- Advertising data, such as ads viewed
- Product interactions, such as app launches or ad interactions
- Crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostics

Google’s processing, retention, and security practices are governed by its policies and applicable consent choices. Learn more from [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. In-app purchases

Anam Pro is offered as an in-app purchase through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment and account information. The App receives product and entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features; we do not receive your full payment-card details. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 7. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate issues, maintain appropriate support records, and meet legal obligations. Do not include sensitive farm, animal, or personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

## 8. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your locally stored livestock records with Google or other third parties. Limited device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic signals may be processed by Google as described above, while purchases and App Store services are processed by Apple.

We expect service providers to handle information under their published privacy commitments and applicable law.

## 9. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you edit or delete them in the App, use **Delete All Local Data**, or delete the App. Copies in device backups or exported files may remain until you delete them from those locations.

Because we do not hold your farm and livestock database on a server, we cannot remotely access or delete that local database. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. You may request deletion of eligible support correspondence by contacting us.

## 10. Security

Use a device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, current iOS updates, and appropriate Apple Account security. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Take care when exporting or sharing records.

## 11. Children’s privacy

Anam is a farm-management tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through an Anam account or backend because the App provides neither. If you believe a child sent personal information through support email, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 12. International processing

Apple and Google may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies and legal safeguards.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and may provide additional notice for material changes.
